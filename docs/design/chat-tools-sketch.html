cl_quick: four chat tools

Find in chat, quick actions on a selection, asking about a result, and reading an answer aloud, with the two small helpers that go with them: ↑ to recall and a right-click menu. Six states at the panel's real size (520 × 580 pt) in the app's look, plus a light variant of the first. Sample content only. Nothing is built.

PROPOSED

  • Find (⌘F) is a slim bar under the header, in the answers area. The window never grows; the answers area gets 36 pt shorter while it is open. Matches are softly marked, the current one strong; ↑ ↓ (or ⌘G and ⇧⌘G) step, Esc or × closes.
  • Quick actions float just above a selection in an answer: Explain, Shorter, Ask about this. The bar sits above the text, so it never covers the selection or the pointer, which is at the end of the drag.
  • Ask about this puts the selection into the field as a quote row, with a quote mark and a small × (no coloured edge), and the caret after it. Long selections show two lines and a ….
  • Read aloud is the last icon of an answer's footer. While it reads, the icon is a stop glyph in the accent colour; pressing it stops. One answer reads at a time.
  • ↑ in an empty field brings back the last message you sent; ↑ again goes further back, ↓ forward. A tiny hint is shown (state 5 asks whether it earns its place).
  • Right-click on an answer shows Copy, then the three actions, then Copy chat as Markdown. The three act on the selection if there is one, otherwise on the whole answer.
