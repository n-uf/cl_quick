cl_quick: small controls that open on hover

The operator asked for "small (mobile like), expand on hover". At rest each control is a tiny phone-style glyph; with the pointer on it, the full labelled bars open as an overlay drawn above everything, anchored to the glyph. Hover, click and scroll them, at 320 and 440 pt, in the real row (● Main · controls · Send). Sample models; the switch gives an account four, three or two families.

  • Nothing moves: the glyphs keep one width for every value; the overlay changes no layout, so the profile key and Send stay put.
  • No flicker: the hover zone is the glyph plus the overlay plus the gap between them, and the overlay closes 150 ms after the pointer leaves that zone.
  • It never covers what you aim at: (a) and (c) open above the glyph, leaving it uncovered; (b) opens in place with the step in use right under the pointer.
  • The cost tint (Fable, Extra, Max) is on the glyph while the step in use costs more, and on that step's bars in the overlay; the dot marks the step in use. Scroll over a glyph steps it; ⌥⌘ arrows stay.
  • In the app the overlay would be a small window of its own (like today's hover tip), since anything drawn in the row's view sits under the field.
