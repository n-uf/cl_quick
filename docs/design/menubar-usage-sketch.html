cl_quick: usage in the menu bar

Two places for the account in use's usage: in the menu, every time it opens, and in the menu bar icon itself. Sample account (alex@example.com), sample numbers; everything is drawn at real size in points and shown enlarged (the menus 2x, the menu bars 1.5x), on a dark and a light menu bar.

  • The numbers are cl_quick's own: Claude Code's get_usage, as the menu reads them today. Nothing new is read.
  • Colour is never the only signal: the amount reads from a shape (height, length, ring) so it works in any look and for colour-blind eyes; colour only warns (amber from 80 %, crimson when full).
  • Full: the countdown to the reset stands beside the icon, as cl_audit's menu bar icon does.

STEP 2

The menu bar icon carries the usage

Each option at no numbers yet, 12 %, 46 %, 68 %, 88 % and full (the fuller of the 5-hour and weekly limits), on a dark menu bar, then a light one. I2 is the clean version of the colour idea: the bubble fills, so the amount reads from its height, and colour arrives only at 80 %.

I1 · Colour only (your first idea)

The bubble keeps its shape and turns green, amber, red as the fuller limit fills. It reads as a band, not an amount: 46 % and 68 % look alike from a distance, red and green are the same grey to a colour-blind eye, and a coloured symbol stands out from every other menu bar icon all day.
