cl_quick: the chat window, redesigned

A design pass on the bar, the chat window, its pickers and the profile menu after build 77. What changes: a 10 pt outer gap that the header, the answers and the field box all share; a 40 pt header with the usage numbers on the right; the address in a capsule with a clear edge; model and thinking as step controls instead of menus, tinted when a choice uses your limits faster; a profile menu without the long footer sentence. Sample profiles, addresses and numbers. Nothing is built; the drag and resize work going on now isn't affected.

BEFORE → AFTER

What's off in build 77

Measured from --render-quick-chat at 440 pt.

Guides

Build 77

  • A thick frame around the field: the box sits 12 pt in from the sides and the bottom (24 px on a Retina screen), on top of its own 8 pt padding, so a band of window shows all round it.
  • Four left edges: the field box at 12 pt, the name at 18, the answers at 20, the field text at about 28.
  • Header crowds the corner: 10 pt from the top under a 22 pt corner radius.
  • Uneven header gaps, and the blur has no edge, so it reads as a smear.
  • Reset times inline: the line changes length as the window narrows.
  • Two type families: a 15.5 pt serif for answers next to sans everywhere else.
  • Model and thinking are menus with a lightbulb, two clicks to change either.

Proposed

  • One 10 pt outer gap on all sides: the field box sits 10 pt from the window's left, right and bottom edges; the header's name and the answers start on the same 10 pt line. Inside the box, 12 pt of padding stays.
  • Header 40 pt high, content centred, corner radius 20 and the box's 12 nested inside it.
  • Identity left, usage right: name, address capsule, space, the meters next to ✎ ✕. Reset times go to the hover card.
  • The address in a capsule: clipped blur with a clear edge at rest, readable on hover.
  • One family: system sans, answers at 14/21.
  • Model and thinking step up and down with one click or one key, no icon; a warm tint when the step costs more.
  • Actions only under the last answer, and on hover under the others.

MODEL AND THINKING NEW

Step controls instead of menus

One click or one key moves a step. The lists come from Claude Code, so a control may have 2 to 6 steps.

Why A: the bars say "more or less" without words, show how far up you are, and give a bigger target than B's chevrons or C's tiny arrows. Hovering a half lights the next bar and the hint names that step, so you see the result before you click. No lightbulb: the bars carry the meaning, and the hint says "Thinking: High". Off is a control with no bar filled.

Uses your limits faster: the tint

A control turns warm when its own step costs more: the model at Fable, or thinking at Extra or Max. When both are costly, both controls take the stronger tint with a thin ember edge. The costly steps' bars are ember even before you reach them, so the top of the scale warns ahead. The hint adds "Uses your limits faster". It's a tint, not a warning colour: nothing turns red.

Variable step counts

The model control has one step per family Claude Code offers, Haiku → Sonnet → Opus → Fable, each the newest (its displayName from the initialize response). A family the plan lacks is left out, so 2 to 4 steps. Thinking is Off plus the chosen model's supportedEffortLevels (Low, Medium, High, Extra, Max), so 1 to 6 steps; the bars are the levels, and Off fills none. When the model changes and the current level isn't offered, thinking moves to the nearest lower one and the hint says so for a moment ("Haiku has no Max: High").

For the builder

QuickChatModelChoice (Sources/Chat.swift:17) loses profile ("Profile's default" goes) and gains fable; the steps are built from the initialize response, not hard-coded. --model takes the aliases haiku, sonnet, opus, fable, so the newest of each comes for free. If a switch is refused (a plan without Fable), the parsed control_response error steps the control back to where it was and the hint shows the reason. QCChip stays for the profile picker only; the model and thinking menus and their footers go.

THE BAR

Before the first message

The bar is the field box on its own.

With no header, the week meter sits next to Send; at 320 it drops its number. The bar has no outer gap of its own: it is the box, with the window's 20 pt radius. When the first answer arrives the window grows above it and the box takes its 10 pt gap.

HEADER

Every state of the line

At rest and with the pointer on it, at the three widths.

The line drops, in orderWhen
1. Reset timesAlways: they're in the card under the header, on hover (decision 2).
2. Labels shorten"session" / "week" become "5h" / "wk", both together.
3. The addressLeaves the line; the hover card shows it on its first row.
4. PercentagesBars only; "full" always stays.
5. LabelsBars alone; a long profile name is the last to give way and is cut with an ellipsis.

Pointing anywhere on the header does two things: the address turns readable in place (the capsule keeps its width, so nothing shifts), and a card appears under the meters, right-aligned to the 10 pt line, with both windows' percentage and reset time. Clicking the name still opens the profile in Settings. Dragging anywhere else on the line moves the window.

PROFILE MENU

The one menu left

Native macOS menu, kept narrow: grey words go under the item, and the footer sentence is gone.

One profile: Main with its week, then Add a profile…, which opens Settings on the + page, so the menu has something to do. Several profiles: a dot in each profile's level colour, its week and reset under the name; a full one stays pickable and says when it frees up. The grey second line is the menu item subtitle (macOS 14.4 and later; on 14.0 to 14.3 it goes to the tooltip; not yet checked against the SDK). What the footer said moves to the picker's tooltip: "For this chat. A new chat starts on the default."

YOUR PICK

Decisions

Reply with one letter per number, e.g. "1A 2A 7A". The recommended option is first. Numbers changed since the first version of this page.

  • Settled by you, drawn as such: a 10 pt outer gap for the field box, header and answers; the field stays a box with comfortable padding inside.
  • Model and thinking are step controls, not menus, with no lightbulb. Model steps: Haiku → Sonnet → Opus → Fable, the newest of each, from Claude Code's list; "Profile's default" goes.
  • Thinking steps: Off, then the model's own levels up to Max. Fable, Extra and Max get a warm tint, stronger when both are costly.
